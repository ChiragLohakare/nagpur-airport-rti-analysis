Nagpur Airport — Operations & Revenue Analysis (FY 2015–16 to FY 2024–25)
An independent data analytics project based on operational data obtained from MIHAN India Limited via the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 for Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur (IATA: NAG).

Project Overview
This project analyzes 10 years of airport performance data across five key operational metrics — passenger traffic, cargo throughput, aircraft movements, aeronautical revenue, non-aeronautical revenue, and capital expenditure — to identify growth trends, performance gaps, and strategic revenue opportunities.

Key Findings

239% revenue growth from FY 2015–16 (₹4,122 L) to FY 2024–25 (₹13,953 L) — CAGR of ~12.9%
68.7% COVID traffic collapse in FY 2020–21, followed by full recovery by FY 2022–23
Non-aeronautical revenue gap — NAR share declined from 33% to 17%, representing a ₹34–42 Cr/year untapped opportunity
Cargo crossed 10,000 MT for the first time in FY 2024–25, with outbound cargo surging 91.7% in 4 years
Aircraft movement plateau at ~25,000 since FY 2018–19, signaling capacity constraints
